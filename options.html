Jev Review

Jev grades your Gmail replies as you write them, and lists the questions you haven't answered yet.

TypeSafe API key

Your draft and the message you're replying to are sent to api.typesafe.ai to be graded.

When to review

You can always review now with Alt+J in the message, or with ↻ on the card.

Criteria

Each criterion is one question Jev answers about your message, graded good / ok / poor. "Replies only" criteria need the message you're answering.
